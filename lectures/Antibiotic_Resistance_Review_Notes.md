# Antibiotic resistance module review record

Version 1.0, September 16, 2026.

Scope: adult bacterial resistance primer for residents, with fellow and attending discussion. Covers diagnostic reasoning, colonization, mechanisms, susceptibility interpretation, ESBL, inducible AmpC, CRE by mechanism, resistant Pseudomonas, CRAB, Stenotrophomonas, MRSA, VRE, dosing, source control, monitoring, oral transition, duration, and transmission prevention. Eight fictional cases, ten explained questions, four study appraisals, 18 source links, and a one-page rounds sheet. Self-contained HTML uses the established module styling without the site sidebar.

Clinical review used the live 2026 IDSA AMR guidance, its dosing and breakpoint tables, the September 9, 2026 IDSA/ESCMID SAB consensus, vancomycin guidance, CDC CRE infection-control guidance, and the linked regulatory records. The 2011 MRSA guideline is labeled as older and interpreted alongside subsequent approvals and consensus statements. BJC pharmacy-directed trough-monitoring wording is retained from the user's established instruction; no institutional policy was independently retrieved.

Specific 2026 changes preserved: ceftriaxone MIC surrogate for ESBL; newer cystitis agents; cefepime-enmetazobactam and IV fosfomycin roles; selected oral fosfomycin transition caveat; Hafnia alvei and the limited ceftriaxone continuation exception for AmpC; fixed aztreonam-avibactam for NDM; ceftolozane-tazobactam preference for DTR Pseudomonas pneumonia; high-dose ampicillin-sulbactam as a temporary CRAB bridge; and cefiderocol monotherapy preference for invasive Stenotrophomonas, with limited human comparative evidence explicitly stated. Laboratory interpretation includes CLSI versus EUCAST categories and the species-specific daptomycin SDD distinction. Guidance-supported uses are distinguished from FDA indications.

Evidence appraisal checks population, endpoint, effect estimate, uncertainty, and limits for MERINO, ALLIUM, ATTACK, and the VA daptomycin-dose cohort. Some original publications were available only as indexed abstracts or regulatory reports; unrestricted full-text access is not claimed. Direct label access succeeded for linezolid after following the manufacturer's prescribing-information link. The inaccessible manufacturer daptomycin URL was replaced with the retrieved 2025 FDA label.

Verification before publication: repository preparation, site validation, all eight existing behavior tests, and RSS generation passed. Chromium rendered desktop (1440 pixels) and mobile (390 pixels) views without document overflow or JavaScript errors. Cases and answers opened correctly; IDs and internal anchors passed inspection. No external scripts, stylesheets, or iframes are required. The complete module prints to 22 Letter pages with answers expanded; the rounds sheet prints to one Letter page and was visually inspected. Rendered desktop and mobile views were visually inspected. QA outputs are temporary and are not part of the site.

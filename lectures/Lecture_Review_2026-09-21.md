# Published lecture review — September 21, 2026

Scope: all ten HTML modules linked from the public lecture catalog. This review concerns the website versions. Unpublished lecture drafts were not added to the catalog.

All ten modules were read for clinical reasoning, internal consistency, relevance to residents and fellows, source attribution, navigation, and presentation. High-impact diagnostic and treatment recommendations were rechecked against available public guidelines, primary publications, and product labeling. The existing faculty, pharmacy, and local-review statuses remain pending; this review does not imply independent clinical sign-off.

## Shared standard

- Full lectures use a 30–45-minute resident discussion with advanced fellow teaching below; short modules retain a 10–20-minute core.
- Every module has a visible link to its advanced or fellow section. Explanations and advanced teaching are visible without opening dropdowns.
- Lecture pages use the red/green teaching palette, a single content column, accessible table regions, and no teaching sidebar.
- Full modules offer separate core, complete-reference, and rounds-sheet print choices. The resistance matrix has its own print option. Browser Print includes the complete module.
- The catalog and module revision dates agree. Historical publication and source-access dates are preserved.
- Existing source anchors, extended discussions, and source-access limitations remain available. No private institutional document, commercial board-preparation content, or source PDF was added to the repository.

What gets included

The focus is practical adult ID: diagnosis, antimicrobial choice, dosing, duration, route, source control, microbiology, follow-up, OPAT, stewardship, and infection prevention.

Social media, journal feeds, and other curated sources are used to find papers. Conclusions are checked against the original publication. HIV is included when there is a meaningful guideline, treatment, prevention, resistance, or safety update.

AI assists with finding papers, screening, and drafting summaries. Entries are checked against the original source before they are added, but errors are possible; use the linked publication for clinical decisions.

Impact labels are editorial, not guideline grades. Read the original publication before applying a finding to patient care.

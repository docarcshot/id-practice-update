What gets included

This site prioritizes new infectious diseases literature that can affect diagnosis, antimicrobial selection, dosing, duration, route, source control, interpretation of microbiology, monitoring, follow-up, OPAT, stewardship, or infection prevention.

Primary sources are verified before inclusion. Social media and curated feeds may identify papers, but they are treated as discovery signals rather than evidence. HIV is included alongside the other major adult ID domains when there is clinically meaningful new guidance, treatment or prevention evidence, resistance or safety information, or another development worth changing or informing practice.

AI is used to assist literature discovery, screening, and drafting of summaries. Primary-source details and included recommendations are verified before publication.

Impact labels are editorial judgments, not formal guideline grades. The summaries are educational and should be read alongside the original publication and patient-specific clinical context.
